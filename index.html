About the Summit

Where Ideas Meet Execution

Flagship'26 is the inaugural event of entrepreneurship Cell of VNIT that aims to foster innovation, entrepreneurship, and leadership among young minds. This event marks the conclave of pioneering personalities that have proven themselves in different walks of life. Elite speakers who have demonstrated their expertise on the international stage and can inspire the crowd with their interactions are invited every year.
